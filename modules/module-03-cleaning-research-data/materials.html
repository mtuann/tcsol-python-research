Chấm theo quyết định cleaning và khả năng giải thích

Grade cleaning decisions and explanatory clarity

CategoryPointsStrong submission
Problem inspection15Identifies missing values, duplicate key, label variants, type failures, and invalid ranges.
Cleaning decisions25Cleans one rule at a time, preserves audit columns, and avoids unjustified filling.
Cleaning log20States issue type, detected count, decision, affected rows, risk, and paper note.
Figure quality15Before/after quality figure is readable, labeled, exported, and linked to the cleaning log.
Methods writing20Explains observation key, row exclusions, conversion rules, and limitation.
Reproducibility5Notebook runs from raw data to outputs without manual edits.

Weak charts often begin with unclean data

ProblemMisleading resultFix
Label variantsOne group becomes several bars.Normalize labels with a map.
Duplicate keyOne learner receives extra weight.Define and check the observation key.
Invalid scoreMean score is inflated.Set impossible values to NA and log the rule.
Silent missingnessSample size changes invisibly.Report complete-pair count.
