# Figure Checklist

Before a figure is paper-ready, check:

- The research question is clear.
- The unit of observation is documented.
- The data source is cited.
- The plot table is saved or reproducible.
- The chart type matches the claim.
- Axes and units are readable.
- Color carries meaning and remains accessible.
- Labels are direct where possible.
- The figure exports to the required format.
- The caption states data, measure, pattern, and limitation.
