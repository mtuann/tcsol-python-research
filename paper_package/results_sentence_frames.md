# Results Sentence Frames

Use these frames to connect figures to paper prose.

- Figure X shows that [group/time/category] has a higher/lower [measure] than [comparison], suggesting [interpretation].
- The distribution in Figure X indicates that [pattern], rather than a single average, is important for interpreting [research issue].
- The relationship in Figure X is consistent with [interpretation], but the data do not support a causal claim.
- The confidence intervals in Figure X suggest [degree of uncertainty], so the result should be treated as [strong/tentative/descriptive].
- After cleaning and recoding, the main pattern remains [pattern], which supports [limited claim].
