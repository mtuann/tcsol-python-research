# Tuần 02 Instructor Notes

## Teaching Intent

Week 2 is not a pandas week. Keep the lesson focused on research data design:

- What does one row mean?
- What columns are required?
- Which columns are optional?
- What privacy risks appear before data collection?

## Pacing Risk

The learner may want to design a full dataset immediately. Keep the core task to one selected track and one table plan.

## Domain Cautions

- TCSOL: do not use identifiable learner data.
- Contrastive: do not claim learner transfer unless the dataset contains learner production.
- MTPE: call Week 2 labels simplified/MQM-inspired, not full MQM annotation.
- Policy: every excerpt needs metadata and access date.

## Week 3 Bridge

Week 3 will turn the table plan into a `pandas.DataFrame` and produce a descriptive table.
