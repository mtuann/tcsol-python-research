# Week 05 Instructor Notes

## Teaching Stance

Keep Week 05 beginner-centered. The learner should not leave thinking visualization means mastering every chart type. The week succeeds if the learner can make two clear figures, export them, and write a cautious caption.

## Emphasis Points

- A figure is a claim with evidence.
- Small data should show individual points or sample size.
- Bar charts are acceptable for summaries, but they can hide variation.
- The caption is part of the figure, not an afterthought.
- Exported files are reproducible artifacts.

## Suggested Live Questions

1. If a reader only sees the bar chart, what do they miss?
2. What changes when we show every learner as a dot?
3. Which words in the interpretation would be overclaiming?
4. Which figure would be most useful for MTPE or education policy data?

## Common Beginner Confusions

- Thinking `savefig` is only for screenshots.
- Thinking the chart title and caption must say the same thing.
- Thinking a high mean proves one activity is better.
- Forgetting to filter to usable rows before plotting.

## Suggested Feedback Sentence

> Your figure is readable and exported correctly. To make it paper-ready, add usable N in the caption and soften the interpretation from causal language to descriptive language.
