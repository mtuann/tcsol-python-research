# Week 06 Rubric

| Criterion | Points | Strong Work |
|---|---:|---|
| Code runs top-to-bottom | 20 | Notebook loads data, filters usable rows, computes all required statistics, and exports outputs. |
| Statistical table | 20 | Table includes `n`, `mean`, `SD`, `SE`, and `95% CI`, with clear variable names. |
| CI figure | 15 | Figure labels groups, shows usable `n`, and displays confidence intervals clearly. |
| Results paragraph | 25 | Paragraph reports estimate, uncertainty, comparison, and limitation without causal overclaim. |
| Interpretation | 10 | Learner distinguishes SD from SE/CI and explains why sample/design limits claims. |
| Reproducibility | 10 | Paths, source note, and generated files are clear enough to rerun. |

Passing threshold: 70/100.
