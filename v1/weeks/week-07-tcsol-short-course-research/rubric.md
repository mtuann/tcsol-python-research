## Learner-Task Rubric Anchor Example

This is not the assignment grade. It is a model for the 1-5 task-performance rubric used in the Week 07 dataset.

| Criterion | Score 1 | Score 3 | Score 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Accuracy | Rarely uses the result complement in the response. | Uses a result complement such as `找到了`, but sometimes places it incorrectly or omits `了`. | Uses result complements accurately across most service-dialogue turns. |
| Confidence | Avoids responding or relies almost entirely on teacher prompt. | Responds after support but hesitates on new prompts. | Responds with little hesitation and can self-correct after a cue. |
