# Week 08 Instructor Notes

## Teaching Timing

- 15 min: review Week 07 bridge from `main_difficulty` to coded response rows.
- 20 min: explain unit of analysis and codebook rule.
- 30 min: live code frequency table and crosstab.
- 20 min: read representative examples and discuss teaching implications.
- 25 min: write Results paragraph and captions.

## Beginner Support

Keep the learner focused on one question at a time:

1. Which rows can be analyzed?
2. Which rows contain coded errors?
3. Which code appears most often?
4. Which target structure shows the pattern?
5. Which example helps the reader understand it?

## Watch For

- The learner may count `no_error` as an error. Pause and explain why no-error rows are useful for data context but excluded from error frequency.
- The learner may treat transfer hypotheses as facts. Require "possible" or "may" language.
- The learner may overfocus on Chinese examples and forget the Results paragraph. Keep the writing output visible from the start.

## Bridge To Week 09

Week 09 can take the representative examples and ask: which patterns are plausibly connected to Chinese-Vietnamese contrastive differences, and what data would support that claim?
