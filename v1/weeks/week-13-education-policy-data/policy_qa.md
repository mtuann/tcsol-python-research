# Week 13 Policy Source QA

## What This Dataset Is

- A classroom dataset for learning policy metadata and coding.
- Built from official/source metadata and short paraphrases.
- Not a complete policy corpus.
- Not evidence of implementation success.

## Before Using A Real Policy Source

Check:

1. Is the issuing body clear?
2. Is the date clear?
3. Is the source URL stable enough for citation?
4. Are you quoting, paraphrasing, or coding?
5. Does the claim describe the document, or evaluate policy effects?

## Safe Claim Frames

- "The source set includes..."
- "Rows coded as `digitalization` appear..."
- "The bulletin provides statistical context, but it does not by itself explain causality."
- "The policy plan states a priority; implementation evidence would require additional data."
