Caption

Caption nói figure đang show gì

Figure 1. Gain score by activity focus in the synthetic Week 05 TCSOL dataset (N = 25 usable records). Panel A shows group means; Panel B shows individual records; the figure is descriptive, not causal.
Interpretation

Viết pattern + limitation trong cùng đoạn

Result complements has the highest descriptive mean gain, while word order has the lowest. Because each group has only 5-8 usable records, the companion dot plot is needed to keep the Results paragraph cautious.
