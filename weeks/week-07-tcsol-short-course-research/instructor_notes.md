# Week 07 Instructor Notes

Keep the session design-first. If the learner gets excited by the charts, bring them back to the core research habit: define task evidence before interpreting numbers.

## Suggested Timing

- 15 minutes: short-course research loop.
- 20 minutes: rubric and variable map.
- 25 minutes: live coding notebook.
- 20 minutes: Methods draft workshop.
- 10 minutes: source note and limitation check.

## Watch For

- Rubric descriptors that are too abstract.
- Confusing `activity_focus` with a causal treatment.
- Methods paragraphs that omit exclusion rules.
- Source notes that name ACTFL/CEFR/Chinese standards but do not explain how they were adapted.
