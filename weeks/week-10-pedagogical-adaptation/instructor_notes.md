# Week 10 Instructor Notes

## Teaching Rhythm

1. Start from Week 09: a contrastive priority is not yet a lesson plan.
2. Ask the learner what a teacher can actually do in 40-45 minutes.
3. Show one activity row and identify the unit of analysis.
4. Run score calculation and pause before sorting.
5. Make the learner explain `sort_values()` as "put high-priority rows first".
6. Use `groupby()` to read pacing: how much time goes to each lesson phase?
7. End by writing a pedagogical implication with a limitation.

## Instructor Answer Key

- Raw activity rows: 25.
- Model short lesson rows: 7.
- Model short lesson minutes: 47.
- Highest activity by adaptation score: `A001` / `Notice action vs result`.
- Main phenomenon selected for the model lesson: `result_complement`.

Keep the answer key out of the learner's first writing attempt.
