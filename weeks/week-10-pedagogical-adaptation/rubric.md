# Week 10 Rubric

| Criterion | Points | Strong Work |
|---|---:|---|
| Data loading and score calculation | 15 | Correctly loads the CSV and computes `adaptation_score` from the documented columns. |
| Ranking with `sort_values()` | 15 | Correctly ranks activities and explains what ranking does and does not prove. |
| Grouping with `groupby()` | 20 | Produces phase-minutes summary and reads it as lesson pacing. |
| Lesson plan table | 15 | Generates and interprets the model sequence with phase, minutes, learner output, and assessment evidence. |
| Figure and caption | 15 | Core figure is readable; caption states variables, unit, 45-minute frame when relevant, and limitation. |
| Pedagogical adaptation writing | 20 | Paragraph connects evidence, activity sequence, teaching implication, limitation, and one checked source note in 130-180 words. |
