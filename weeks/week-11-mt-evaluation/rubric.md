# Week 11 Rubric

| Criterion | Points | Strong work |
|---|---:|---|
| Unit of analysis | 15 | Clearly separates source, MT output, and reference; states 12 source segments and 36 system-segment rows. |
| Metric computation | 20 | Computes BLEU, chrF++, and TER by system with correct score directions and exports metric settings. |
| Human review | 20 | Summarizes review decisions and severity without treating `no_error` or `acceptable_variant` as errors. |
| Figure | 15 | Caption states metric, system profiles, N, score direction, and limitation. |
| Writing | 20 | Results paragraph separates metric result, human review, and limitation in 130-180 words. |
| Source note | 10 | Cites metric/MQM source with link, access date, and synthetic-data caveat. |

Stretch work can add segment-level disagreement analysis, but it should not replace the Core outputs.
