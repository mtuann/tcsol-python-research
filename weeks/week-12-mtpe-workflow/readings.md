# Week 12 Readings

## Required, Light Core

1. RapidFuzz Levenshtein documentation. Focus only on what `distance(a, b)` returns.
   https://rapidfuzz.github.io/RapidFuzz/Usage/distance/Levenshtein.html

2. ISO 18587:2017 overview. Focus only on the phrase "post-editing of machine translation output" and the idea that full human post-editing is a managed process.
   https://www.iso.org/standard/62970.html

3. Sun, Wang, and Jia (2025), "Direction matters: Comparing post-editing and human translation effort and quality." Focus on the idea that post-editing effort can be studied empirically, not on the statistical details.
   https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0328511

## Optional / Instructor Source Menu

- Scarton et al. (2019), Estimating post-editing effort.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.06204

- TAUS MT post-editing guidelines.
  https://www.taus.net/resources/reports/mt-post-editing-guidelines

- ViBidirectionMT-Eval (2025), Vietnamese bidirectional MT evaluation context.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.08621
