# Week 13 Rubric

| Criterion | Points | Strong work |
|---|---:|---|
| Deliverable completeness | 10 | Submits correctly named policy-area summary, source-type summary, timeline table, figure, caption, source note, and Data/Methods paragraph. |
| Metadata accuracy | 15 | Clearly records title, issuing body, date, URL, and access date. |
| Timeline processing | 15 | Converts dates correctly, validates invalid dates, and explains publication dates vs access-date placeholders. |
| Coding scheme use | 20 | Counts policy areas/evidence types and explains what the codes mean. |
| Source caution | 15 | Separates policy description, statistics, metadata, and evaluation claims. |
| Figure and caption | 10 | Figure is readable; caption states rows, source types, and limitation. |
| Writing and source note | 15 | Data/Methods paragraph is 130-180 words, names unit/metadata/coding/date basis, and source note cites official/method sources with access date. |

Stretch work can add the theme-by-source crosstab and a short codebook revision note.
