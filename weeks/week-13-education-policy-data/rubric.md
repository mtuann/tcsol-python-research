# Week 13 Rubric

| Criterion | Points | Strong work |
|---|---:|---|
| Deliverable completeness | 10 | Submits correctly named summary table, timeline table, figure, caption, source note, and Results paragraph. |
| Metadata accuracy | 15 | Clearly records title, issuing body, date, URL, and access date. |
| Timeline processing | 15 | Converts dates correctly and sorts rows in a meaningful order. |
| Coding scheme use | 20 | Counts policy areas/evidence types and explains what the codes mean. |
| Source caution | 15 | Separates policy description, statistics, metadata, and evaluation claims. |
| Figure and caption | 10 | Figure is readable; caption states rows, source types, and limitation. |
| Writing and source note | 15 | Results paragraph is bounded; source note cites official/method sources and access date. |
